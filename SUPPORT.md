# Support

Something not working, or think you've found a change that shouldn't be there? Email **rcsoftlabs@gmail.com**. We usually reply within a few days.

It helps if you include your Mac model, your macOS version, and what happened just before the problem. In the game, **Pause › Copy details for a bug report** copies the version, your Mac and your settings, ready to paste into the email.

## How to play

- Floor 1 is the reference. It never has a change of its own, so walk around and learn it.
- On every other floor, explore the lobby and the corridor, then go back into the lift:
  - **Nothing changed:** press **UP**.
  - **Anything is different:** photograph it with the camera, then press **DOWN**. If it's a sound, photograph the room it comes from.
- The lift answers when its doors open on the next floor. A wrong call takes you back to floor 1. Reach the roof to check out.
- On floors 2 to 9 the lift only waits 40 seconds. If you'd rather take your time, choose **Relaxed stay** on the title screen: no countdown.
- Some changes are sounds, and some only happen after a while or when you get close. Sound captions are in Settings.
- Check out once and **Orbital** opens on the title screen: a second stay on the decks of the hotel's other address, where the lift waits 55 seconds a deck.

## Hints and the hint unlock

- You get 5 free hints. A hint tells you exactly what changed on the current floor. Use one: press R, or Pause › Hints.
- The one-time **Unlock All Hints** purchase unlocks all hints for good, on every Mac signed in to the same Apple Account.
- **Restore a purchase:** Pause › Hints › **Restore Purchases**. You'll need to be signed in to the App Store with the Apple Account you bought it with.
- **Refunds** are handled by Apple: go to [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

## Common questions

**Is anything ever on floor 1?**
Floor 1 never gets a change of its own. But a change you miss can stay, on floor 1 too, until three right calls in a row settle it. Arriving on floor 1, the lift tells you what is still changed.

**I'm sure something changed, but the game said I was wrong.**
Your photo has to show the change. Some changes are subtle: a number, a direction, a sound, a clock. If your photo missed, the verdict says which room the change was in, and a hint tells you what it was.

**Reset my progress.**
Quit the game, then delete the folder `~/Library/Containers/com.rcsoftlabs.alderlift/`. This doesn't remove your purchase: use Restore Purchases afterwards.

**It runs slowly.**
Close other apps and make sure your Mac is plugged in. Settings › Resolution set to Auto keeps large screens smooth. If it's still slow, email us your Mac model and macOS version.

**System requirements**
macOS 12.0 or later, on a Mac with Apple silicon or an Intel Mac that supports Metal.

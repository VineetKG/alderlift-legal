# Privacy Policy

**The Alder Lift** (the "Game") is published by **Vineet Garg** ("we", "us").
Effective date: **21 November 2026**

## The short version

**We don't collect any personal data.** The Game has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It doesn't send anything about you to us or to anyone else.

## What stays on your Mac

The Game saves a small file on your Mac so it can remember your progress. It holds the changes you've seen, your best times, your achievements, how many free hints you've used, and your settings. Beside it, a folder keeps the photos you've taken for the guest register. Both live in the Game's own sandbox folder:

`~/Library/Containers/com.rcsoftlabs.alderlift/Data/`

They never leave your Mac, unless your own backups (such as Time Machine) copy them. To delete them, delete the Game and that folder.

## Purchases

The Game offers one optional in-app purchase that unlocks all hints. **Apple processes the purchase.** We never see your name, Apple Account, or payment details. The Game asks the App Store on your Mac whether the purchase has been made, so it can unlock the hints and restore them on your other Macs. Apple's handling of purchase data is covered by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Crash reports

If you've chosen in macOS to share analytics with app developers (System Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements), Apple may share anonymous crash reports and usage statistics about the Game with us. We use them only to fix bugs. You can turn this off at any time in the same place.

## Emails to us

If you email us for support, we use your email address and your message only to reply to you. We don't add you to any mailing list. We keep the conversation only as long as we need it to help you; ask us to delete it at any time.

## Children

The Game isn't directed at children under 13. It collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

## Third parties

The Game contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking code. It's built with the open-source Godot Engine, which doesn't send any data.

## Your rights

Because we don't hold personal data about you, there's usually nothing to access, correct or delete. If you've emailed us, you can ask for a copy or deletion of that correspondence at rcsoftlabs@gmail.com. Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK under the GDPR, or California under the CCPA/CPRA), you may also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it on this page and change the effective date above. If the Game ever starts collecting data, we'll say so here and in its App Store privacy label **before** that version is released.
